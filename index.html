The Artist

Engineer by Training.
Artist by Soul.

I'm Onosedere Flex Voke — known as Flex Voke.

I started drawing when I was 10 years old. At that time, it wasn’t because I had a plan to become an artist; I was just doing it because I was a child who simply enjoyed drawing. I’m self-taught, actually.
I’m a certified mechanical engineer turned full-time career artist and house styling/decorating creator. Through ArtSIde, we bring art to walls, ceilings, homes, offices, and public spaces across Nigeria and beyond.

"Art and styling have a beautiful way of transforming not just walls, but the spirit of the space — and the people who experience it."
Self-Taught Mural Artist Interior Stylist Mechanical Engineer ARTSIDE Founder

What We Do

Art That Lives
In Your Space.

From intimate bedroom murals to sprawling commercial walls — every project is approached with full dedication and a painter's eye for story.

Category 01

Ceiling Murals

Look up and be moved. Voke brings epic, ceiling-to-cornice murals that turn the fifth wall into your most dramatic statement. Whether it's classical warriors on horseback or celestial dreamscapes — the ceiling becomes the crown of your space.

Category 02

Wall Murals

Walls are canvases. Voke works across every genre — bold Afrocentric figures, pop-art portraits, lush nature scenes, vibrant abstract compositions. Each mural is custom-designed for the room and the story the client wants to tell.

Category 03

Kids' Room Murals

Give a child a room they'll never want to leave. Voke paints the heroes, worlds, and adventures children love — from superheroes flying across cityscape walls to magical forests. Vivid, safe, and built to last.

Category 04

Commercial & Exterior Murals

Businesses, restaurants, event centres, and public spaces trust ARTSIDE to create murals that become landmarks. Large-scale, weatherproof, and designed to make your brand and space unforgettable.

Category 05

Fine Art & Canvas

Beyond walls, Voke creates standalone fine art pieces — rich in detail, symbolism, and emotion. Commissions available for homes, offices, and collectors who want original work that speaks.
